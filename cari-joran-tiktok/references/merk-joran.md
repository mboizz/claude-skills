## Kata kunci jenis
- spinning, casting / baitcasting (BC), UL / ultralight, ML, MH, heavy
- jigging, slow pitch, popping, surf casting / joran pantai, joran tegek, joran tegeg,
  joran kolam, joran galatama, joran trolling, joran sambung vs joran ruas (tele)
- bahan: carbon, karbon, fiber, solid, hollow; action: fast, extra fast, moderate
