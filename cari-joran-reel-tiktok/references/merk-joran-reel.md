# Referensi kata kunci joran & reel pancing (pasar Indonesia)

Daftar ini titik awal pencarian, bukan rekomendasi. Seri bisa berubah — konfirmasi dari hasil pencarian.

## JORAN

### Merk yang sering dibahas
- **Lokal / populer di marketplace**: Daido, Kenzi, Exori, Pioneer, Maguro, Relix Nusantara,
  Lure Bandit, Kaizo, Seahawk, Seaknight, Pro Hunter, Gladiator, NBS.
- **Jepang**: Shimano, Daiwa, Major Craft, Tailwalk, Jackall; **Swedia/AS**: Abu Garcia.
- **China / OEM**: Kastking, Kingdom, Haibo, Goture, Sougayilang, Mavllos, Kuying, Tsurinoya.
- **Premium**: Penn, St. Croix, G. Loomis, Ugly Stik (Shakespeare).

### Kata kunci jenis
spinning, casting / baitcasting (BC), UL / ultralight, ML, MH, heavy, jigging, slow pitch,
popping, surf casting / joran pantai, joran tegek, joran kolam, joran galatama, trolling,
joran sambung vs joran tele; bahan carbon/karbon, fiber, solid; action fast/extra fast/moderate.

## REEL

### Merk yang sering dibahas
- **Lokal / populer di marketplace**: Daido, Kenzi, Exori, Pioneer, Maguro, Relix Nusantara,
  Seahawk, Pro Hunter, Ryobi (populer di Indonesia).
- **Jepang**: Shimano (Sienna, FX, Sedona, Nasci, Miravel, Vanford, Stradic, Twin Power,
  SLX, Curado), Daiwa (Revros, Crossfire, LT series, Fuego, BG, Tatula, Saltiga).
- **Swedia/AS**: Abu Garcia (Black Max, Pro Max, Revo), Penn (Battle, Spinfisher, Slammer, Fathom).
- **China / OEM**: Kastking, Kingdom, Haibo, Deukio, Lizard, Seaknight, Tsurinoya, Piscifun.

### Kata kunci jenis
reel spinning, reel BC / baitcasting (low profile / round), reel overhead, reel power handle (PH),
lever drag, reel jigging, reel slow jigging, reel surf / longcast, reel UL, reel fly;
istilah lain: gulungan, katrol, spool dangkal (shallow) vs dalam (deep), HG/XG (gear tinggi),
drag test, rem magnet, rem sentrifugal, sealed / anti air asin.

### Ukuran reel (patokan umum)
- Spinning 500–1000: UL, kolam/sungai kecil. 2000–3000: casting ringan, serbaguna.
  4000–5000: laut ringan, pantai. 6000–10000+: jigging, popping, ikan besar.
- BC 100–150: casting ringan–sedang; 200–300: laut/ikan besar.
